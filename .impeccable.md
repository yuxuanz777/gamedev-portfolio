# Portfolio design context

This context is derived from the current site content and the owner's requested portfolio updates. It is used only for local design exploration.

## Audience

- Game-studio recruiters, hiring managers, combat-design leads, and technical game-development collaborators.
- They need to understand the candidate's role, shipped/industry context, strongest work, and technical range within one short scan.

## Primary goals

1. Establish Seven / 张宇瑄 as a combat designer who can also implement systems.
2. Put NetEase Leihuo and the MMO project context near the top.
3. Make Return to Azeroth the lead project and use the original Shimmer cover.
4. Keep the English and Chinese experiences equivalent.
5. Preserve direct paths to projects, resume, and contact.

## Brand and tone

- Premium, authored, imaginative, and grounded in real game-development work.
- Fantasy-world atmosphere is welcome, but readability and evidence come before decoration.
- Existing gold, charcoal, teal, project imagery, and portrait are valid brand material.
- Avoid generic SaaS gradients, glass-card soup, invented metrics, and decorative motion without purpose.

## Constraints

- Vue 3 + TypeScript + Less; do not migrate frameworks for a visual concept.
- Reuse local assets. Do not generate replacement game art.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and keyboard focus.
- Design-lab routes must exist only in Vite development mode, so experiments cannot enter a production build accidentally.
